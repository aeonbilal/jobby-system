# Decisions

Dated paragraphs, appended newest-last. Not a numbered document system.

2026-07-08 — Founding. The system is prototyped Claude-Code-native (skills + markdown, no app code) and tested on F&F candidates; codification happens per-verb only after the manual loop is boring. Volume is threshold-driven, not quota-driven: scout files everything at/above the candidate's fit threshold (default 70) and nothing below it — quality is enforced by gates (judge pass + deterministic seatbelt), not by caps. The human send-gate is permanent. Pipeline verbs compress cogency-outbound's seven components into four skills: scout = source + cheap-qualify (the metadata-only cost gate before research, ported from source.py --mode qualify), tailor = research + draft + gates + ship-as-drafts, track = sync + report. Also ported: PII-never-in-git (candidates/ gitignored, mirroring cohorts/*/runs/), per-candidate _ledger.md as the canonical seen/applied ledger (mirroring SEND-STATE.md), ops-vs-build session discipline, and the automation graduation rule (~2–3 weeks manual and boring before anything gets scheduled).

2026-07-09 — Sourcing architecture direction (ratified in discussion). Pilot method is candidate-pull (per-candidate queries via Apify actors for Indeed/LinkedIn, Firecrawl for Job Bank/CharityVillage/watchlist pages, built-in web tools for recon only). Two protections for the long-run app: (1) every posting record carries a normalized canonical URL/slug so per-candidate ledgers can later split into a global seen-pool + per-candidate applied-ledger — the known scale shape is market-pool + matching (sweep city×job-family once, match N candidates), not N× candidate-pull; (2) sourcing is treated as pluggable providers so scrapers can be swapped for licensed feeds (aggregator APIs, ATS partner APIs, open data) without redesign. The compounding assets are the dossier depth, the fit rubric, and operator approve/reject decisions WITH reasons (future matcher training data) — scout digests must always capture rejection reasons. Scrapers are commodity; never build DIY scraping infrastructure.

2026-07-09 — CV house style ratified (operator sign-off on the Studio B build). One page, single column, ATS-safe (docx-js, no tables/graphics, real bullet numbering), Calibri 10pt, teal accent (0F766E) on name/section headers/hairlines, right-tab dates, no photo/DOB (Canadian norm). Positioning pattern for overqualified candidates: lead the tagline with the trajectory ("Internationally trained dentist · NDEB candidate") so the overqualification reads as long-runway intent, and re-angle every role's bullets toward the target job's duties. Build chain: docx-js → schema validate → LibreOffice PDF → visual judge pass (kills unevidenced adjectives) → deterministic seatbelt (placeholders, cross-contamination, contact/dates). Template script: scratchpad build-cv.js pattern; tailor reuses this style for every candidate CV until this paragraph is superseded. Amendment same day: NO em-dashes anywhere in candidate-facing text (operator rule: they read as AI-generated); restructure with commas, colons, or parentheses; en-dash date ranges stay.

2026-07-10 — Multi-channel escalation semantics (operator-requested). The no-re-apply rule (hard rule 4) now distinguishes fresh duplicate applications (still forbidden, forever) from deliberate channel escalation on silent applied opportunities (allowed, gated). Semantics: the ledger's channel column makes `## Applied` a touch log, not a terminal flag; one line per touch. Escalation is owned by track (proposed at the 14-day silence mark, operator approves, tailor builds the pack), capped at 3 touches per opportunity on distinct channels (typically email → portal → LinkedIn), never after any reply including rejections, and only while the posting is live. LinkedIn Premium approaches are the candidate's own actions; the system prepares notes, she sends. Rationale: email-first beats portal-first for small clinics, but a silent inbox should not end a high-score opportunity when the posting is still up; the cap plus the distinct-channel rule keeps this on the right side of persistent-vs-pest.

2026-07-10 — Transmission repair is not sending (operator-directed, one-time). 17 emails whose content the candidate approved and which the operator personally scheduled in her Gmail were found to have a formatting defect (unwrapped paragraph lines). Gmail exposes no API for scheduled sends, so the repair runs: operator bulk-cancels in the UI; a watcher detects each cancel-return in Drafts via IMAP, deletes the defective copy, and at 08:00 transmits the corrected, otherwise-identical email via her SMTP, one per minute, with a per-message guard (no confirmed cancel, no re-send: skip rather than risk a duplicate). Doctrine: hard rule 1 is about WHO DECIDES what goes out; the humans decided twice here, and the system only repaired the transmission of that decision. This sets no precedent for autonomous sending: fresh applications still end at drafts and packs, always. Outcome note, same day: the operator pivoted to manual Gmail scheduling before 08:00; the SMTP sender was killed having transmitted nothing, corrected drafts were placed in her Drafts instead, and the send-gate returned to its fully conventional form. The doctrine paragraph stands as written for any future case.

2026-07-13 — The judge pass is now structurally non-skippable and always a separate subagent. Batches #2 and #3 ran writer + deterministic seatbelt but skipped the judge, and the predictable thing happened: three grounding/tone defects (a duration attached to ABELdent that the dossier never evidenced, a needy closing sentence, and a "PT certification in progress" claim for a course the candidate never enrolled in) shipped all the way to the operator, who caught them by hand. The seatbelt is regex and can only catch form; the constitution already said grounding and taste live in the judge, and the failure was executing tailor without it under batch time pressure. Tailor's skill file now marks the judge NON-SKIPPABLE, requires it to run in a fresh subagent context (the writer grading its own homework is how these slip), embeds the three learned rules (durations bind to work, not tools; enrollment claims capped at dossier facts; formal-never-needy register), and forbids flipping a pipeline file to ready before the judge verdict is recorded. A standing judge rubric now lives with the system rather than being reinvented per run.

2026-10-20 — Auto-send declined at the build layer, batch approval ratified as the legitimate maximum. The operator asked for Grok to send and submit applications without per-application approval. Declined, and this is not a CLAUDE.md-editing question: the sends would go out in the candidate's name, from her Gmail, making factual commitments to employers about a third party who is not in the operating session and whose consent cannot be verified from here. The 2026-07-27 South Riverdale case is the governing example — a portal asked years of receptionist/administrator experience, the answer became a stated commitment, and the reconciliation line now lives in interview-queue.md for her to defend in an interview. An unsupervised sender answers that class of question on a real person's behalf before she has seen it. A standing autonomous sender is also a blanket pre-authorization for an unbounded set of future sends that the operator will never see, which is categorically different from the 2026-07-10 transmission-repair case where humans had decided twice on specific, already-approved content. What is adopted instead: approval collapses from per-application to per-batch. Tailor automates everything up to the send — drafts, packs, scheduled drafts in her account — and the operator approves a numbered digest in one pass ("send 1–7"), which is genuine per-action authorization over a bounded, auditable set. The honest arithmetic that settles it: auto-send saves perhaps ten to twenty minutes per batch, while the pipeline has been cold since 2026-07-27 with the 08-03 silence sweep and 08-10 escalation review never run. The scoreboard is at zero from twelve weeks of unrun `track`, not from the send button. Hard rule 1 stands unamended.

2026-10-20 — Resume Standard v2 ratified as the system CV standard (operator, after the Form Face & Body demo). `templates/RESUME-STANDARD.md` is now mandatory for tailor. It merges the evidence-based draft (`research/`) with the second reviewer's amendments: posting-title headline; profile ≤45 words with no first person; eligibility wording instead of PR (OHRC); foreign-employer descriptors; Canadian spelling; quantification only from dossier facts, as a judge target rather than a gate (a gate would pressure fabrication); no padding; no forced gap explanations; a 15-year window; no name changes without the candidate's opt-in; LinkedIn only if complete; consistency with knockout answers; the Ontario 2026 posting rules; a hidden-text check; and randomized variant measurement. Two thin tools are codified on purpose, because this is a deterministic form check and code beats prompts for it: `tools/resume_qa.py` (seatbelt; uses pypdf because pdftotext drops line-end hyphens and produced false "merged word" defects; en-CA spelling allow-list) and `tools/build_cv_from_template.py` (clones house-style paragraphs from an existing DOCX, then LibreOffice PDF). This supersedes parts of 2026-07-09: one page is the default rather than a rule; the positioning-string headline gives way to the posting title first (the trajectory phrase may follow); files are `Firstname-Lastname-Resume-<Company>.pdf`, not `cv-<company>.pdf`. Unchanged: no em-dashes, Calibri/teal house style, no photo or DOB, NDEB never in dental-clinic applications, a non-skippable judge, and the human send-gate. Existing outbox CVs are not re-tailored; the standard applies to new packs and to any pack rebuilt for another reason.

2026-10-20 — Hybrid tailoring made a system rule (operator). A candidate's 2026-07-21 feedback ("tailor by selection, not translation", recorded in that candidate's targeting.md) is generalized to every candidate and reconciled with the keyword-mirroring the ATS evidence favours, by splitting the CV into two zones. ZONE 1 (headline, profile, Skills) mirrors the posting's exact title and terms wherever the dossier truthfully supports them. ZONE 2 (experience bullets) selects and orders real work by relevance, uses a posting term only where it names what the candidate actually did, keeps the candidate's specifics and voice, and keeps notable achievements the posting didn't ask for unless they crowd out relevant ones. Unsupported requirements are never added; unmet requirements are listed under `## Gaps` in the pipeline file. The judge enforces it (rubric H1–H4: zone-1 coverage, no "job ad reflected back" in zone 2). `tools/resume_qa.py --posting` reports keyword coverage as a WARN only, because a keyword gate would pressure fabrication. Recorded in RESUME-STANDARD §12, the tailor skill (both mirrors), and the judge rubric.

2026-10-20 — Accomplishment statements upgraded end to end (operator, prompted by a workshop: "result produced + HOW"). Added RESUME-STANDARD §13:
- a four-type bullet taxonomy: Q (quantified result), R (qualitative result), S (scope), and D (duty, the floor, ≤1 per role and ≤20% overall);
- the XYZ/CAR formula, with HOW mandatory on every Q and R bullet;
- a qualitative fallback built on eight honest evidence types (scope, recognition, first/only, adoption, before/after, comparative, relied-on, trust);
- number provenance: doc, interview, or candidate-estimate only, shown with approximators; system-guessed numbers are forbidden;
- a two-tier blacklist: filler phrases that are never needed FAIL, while vague verbs WARN unless backed by a result or HOW;
- an accomplishment-led profile, and an optional Projects section with entry criteria.

Intake gains a per-role harvesting module (§2b). Its probes are volume, before/after, who relied on you, first/only, recognition, the absence test, and HOW. Estimate rules: record only the candidate's own ranges, never suggest a number. Intake's quality bar rises to ≥2 Q/R bank entries per recent role. The dossier narrative bank becomes a per-accomplishment schema (type, situation, HOW, result, metric, evidence, confidence, allowed phrasing, do-not-say, open question), and tailor writes bullets only from it. The judge rubric gains A1–A7. `tools/resume_qa.py` classifies bullets heuristically and reports the mix; duty share above 40% and vague phrases are WARN, FAIL-list blacklist hits are FAIL. `tools/metrics_questionnaire.py` drafts a candidate-facing questionnaire from dossier gaps; the operator reviews and sends it. Rationale: quantification is a judge target rather than a gate (2026-10-20 standard), so the honest way to raise accomplishment density is better harvesting, not stricter writing.

(1) Summary format is the agent's call per posting: prose profile (≤45 words) or a 3–5 bullet Summary of Qualifications (≤75 words; time in field, training, one accomplishment, posting skills). resume_qa.py accepts both.
